Questions we get asked

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace becomes read-only until a plan is activated. Nothing is deleted; you can export at any time.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing period.

Are there per-user or per-transaction fees?

No. A plan has a user and branch capacity; inside it there are no extra charges.

What does the on-server licence include?

A perpetual right to run the covered releases, plus a first year of maintenance. If maintenance lapses you keep running what you have, with full access.

Can we move from hosted to our own server?

Yes, in either direction. The export/import tool moves a whole company, and passwords are reset locally.
