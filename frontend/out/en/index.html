Point of sale

A till that never stops selling

Scan or search, weigh by the kilo, discount a line or the ticket, take cash or bank transfer. If the connection drops, sales are saved on the device and synced when it is back — exactly once.

  • Barcodes, packs and weighed goods
  • Held carts and cash shifts with a counted drawer
  • Works through an outage, no duplicates on sync

Inventory

Stock you can trust to the unit

Every movement — sale, receipt, transfer, adjustment, return — posts to one ledger, so on-hand is always derivable. Batches and expiry dates sell first-expiry-first, and reorder points warn before the shelf is empty.

  • Batches, expiry and FEFO
  • Multi-warehouse, transfers and periodic counts
  • Low-stock and expiry alerts every morning

Collections

Know who owes you, and since when

Credit sales, partial payments and returns land on one customer ledger. Overdue balances surface on the dashboard, statements print in a click, and every payment needs the right approval.

  • Customer statements and ageing
  • Cash, bank transfer with proof, partial payments
  • Approval thresholds for large payments

Common questions

Does it work without internet?

The point of sale keeps selling from cached products; sales are saved on the device and synced when the connection returns. The on-server edition needs no internet at all.

Is it in Arabic?

Fully — right-to-left layout, Arabic documents and receipts, with an English switch on every screen.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export a whole company at any time, or run the on-server edition where the database is on your own machine.

Can we start small?

Yes. A single shop starts on the smallest plan and turns into a multi-branch business by changing a setting, not the product.
