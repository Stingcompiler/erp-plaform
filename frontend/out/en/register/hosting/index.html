Cloud or your own server?

Both run the same Vezano. The difference is where it lives, who looks after it, and how you pay.

Hosted (cloud)

The fastest way to start: a workspace ready within a day of approval.

  • Nothing to install: sign in from a phone, tablet or PC in the browser, or install it as an app.
  • The point of sale keeps selling when the internet drops and syncs when it returns.
  • Updates, backups and security are handled by us; you never run maintenance.
  • Monthly or yearly subscription per plan; add branches, users and devices as you grow.
  • Bank-app payments (Bankak, Fawri, O-Cash) and WhatsApp notifications work out of the box.

Best for: shops, pharmacies, wholesalers and distributors who want to start now with no IT team.

On your own server

Vezano installed on a server your company owns, in your shop, warehouse or data centre.

  • Your data never leaves your premises; it runs even if your internet or our platform is down.
  • One perpetual licence instead of a subscription, quoted for your size.
  • Needs an Ubuntu server (or a small PC that stays on) and someone to keep it powered and backed up.
  • We provide the installation guide and support; updates are applied when you choose.
  • No public storefront page or platform-hosted features: it is a private, in-house system.

Best for: companies with their own IT staff, unreliable connectivity, or a policy that data must stay in-house.
